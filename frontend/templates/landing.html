The Problem

Traditional Packaging Is Destroying Our Environment

Industries and e-commerce rely heavily on non-biodegradable, expensive materials — causing massive environmental damage with no intelligent way to evaluate alternatives.

🏭

High CO₂ Emissions

Conventional packaging materials like expanded polystyrene and plastic wraps produce significant carbon emissions during production and disposal.

Packaging accounts for ~5% of global CO₂ emissions
💸

Cost Inefficiency

Businesses overspend on packaging without visibility into cost-effective eco-friendly alternatives that could save money while reducing environmental impact.

Companies waste up to 30% on suboptimal packaging
🔍

No Decision Support

There is no intelligent system that compares materials across durability, biodegradability, cost, and emissions — leaving businesses guessing.

Zero AI tools exist for packaging material selection
How It Works

From Product Details to Optimal Packaging in Seconds

EcoPackAI's ML pipeline analyzes your product attributes and returns ranked, sustainable packaging recommendations instantly.

1

Select product category, weight, fragility level, and optional budget constraint.
